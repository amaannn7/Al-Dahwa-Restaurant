**Dish photos are the restaurant's own**, taken from its official QR menu (aldafwa.yallaqrcodes.com, linked from its Linktree) on 21 Nov 2026 and stored in `src/assets/menu/` (54 dishes, named by dish id). The originals had black letterbox bands baked in (most files were about two-thirds black), so the bands were cropped off and the remaining photo upscaled 2x with Upscayl's Standard model. Other dishes without a photo show a typographic tile.
